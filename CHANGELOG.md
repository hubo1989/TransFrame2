# Changelog

## 2.0.0 (unreleased)

First release of the rebuilt app. Highlights versus 1.x:

- Translations are drawn on the screenshot (below the original or replacing it) and every translation is written to history.
- One renderer for screen, clipboard, files, pins and thumbnails — what you see is what you export.
- Full screen captures the display under the pointer; every display is frozen at the shortcut.
- Fallback chain of up to five engines, or compare 2–4 engines side by side without re-translating.
- Local privacy guard before any cloud request; redactions are always burned in.
- Structured recognition, smart actions, glossary, tone rewrite, semantic history search and content-aware names.
- Interface in ten languages with instant switching.

TransFrame 2 is a separate app and does not migrate 1.x data. In the editor, `Enter` copies and closes and `⌘S` saves; digits
1–9 pick tools in toolbar order.
